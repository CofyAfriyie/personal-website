About02

From ambiguous question to defensible answer.

I work where data meets operations. My background spans health insurance, business and data analytics, warehouse management and personal assistant work, alongside actuarial science, finance, business intelligence and systems thinking. That mix means I understand how a business actually runs, not only how its numbers look.

I help teams define the rules, organise the information, build the checks, and shape an output that supports responsible action.

The thread across my work is practical governance. A useful model should explain its assumptions. A workflow should make ownership clear. A report should show the gaps, the stale inputs and the unresolved records instead of smoothing them over.

Company03

Data and decision intelligence

CA Data Hub: clear reporting for operations

CA Data Hub is my company and has its own website, separate from this one.

CA Data Hub is an early-stage venture I am building to help businesses turn scattered operational data into clear reporting and practical decisions: analytics, business intelligence, data-quality checks, and focused automation where it is reliable.

The first step is a short conversation about one reporting problem, then a small, scoped piece of work, not a large system. Scope, data access, confidentiality, and fees are agreed before anything starts.

Product mechanics, private workflows, source data, and unpublished concepts remain confidential.
