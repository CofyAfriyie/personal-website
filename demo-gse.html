      Limits

      • One session is shown. The underlying pipeline holds a longer history, but this page makes no claim about trends or performance.
      • Corporate actions (for example splits or bonus issues) can change how prices compare across dates; this prototype does not adjust for them.
      • The share list reflects identities the pipeline has confirmed, not necessarily every instrument the exchange lists.
